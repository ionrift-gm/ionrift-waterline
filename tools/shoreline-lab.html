💡 The Core Game Engine Technique

Video games like Sea of Thieves and Zelda solve arbitrary shorelines via a Signed Distance Field (SDF). Every pixel knows its exact distance to the shore (d) and the shore normal vector (∇d).

Waves organically curve parallel to any winding river or jagged bay, compress as depth decreases (shoaling), and break into cellular Voronoi foam at the swash line.

Why v1 AABB failed on organic shores:
  • AABB distance is calculated against rectangular box borders (x=0, y=0).
  • Waves only move purely horizontally or vertically.
  • Irregular river bends, bays, and coves get flat waves intersecting land sideways.
  • SDF replaces this with exact Euclidean distance to the organic polygon boundary.
